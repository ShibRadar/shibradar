Privacy policy

ShibRadar is an automated account that publishes public information about the Shiba Inu (SHIB) ecosystem: on-chain burns and large transfers on Ethereum, public news headlines and public market data.

ShibRadar does not collect, store or sell personal data of its readers. The only account it accesses through the Threads API is its own (@ShibRadar), and only to publish posts.

Wallet addresses shown in posts are public blockchain data.

Data deletion

ShibRadar keeps no user data. If you believe a post concerns you, contact the account @ShibRadar on Threads and it will be removed.
